# SCR-090 — Profile

Status: Draft
Last updated: 2026-11-06

| Address | Who | Phase | Requirements |
|---|---|---|---|
| `/me` | everyone | P2 | ARC-200 |

## 1. Purpose
Let a user manage their own account.

## 2. Content
- Display name, e-mail (read only), role and organization (read only).

## 3. Actions
- Change display name; change password; sign out of other sessions. MFA set-up comes with ARC-200 (P4).

## 4. States and errors
- Password rules shown on error.
- Common rules: `docs/06-ux/screens/README.md` §2.
